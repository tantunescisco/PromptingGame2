## Quick Snapshot

Prompt Quest 2 teaches prompt engineering as a progression of increasingly powerful habits. Instead of reading theory first, you learn by practicing: compare prompts, improve weak prompts, add structure, and notice how small wording changes produce better AI behavior.

## The Learning Path

Each era focuses on a different prompt skill:

| Level | Focus |
|-------|-------|
| 1 | Clarity, specificity, and saying exactly what you want |
| 2 | Audience, context, scope, and useful constraints |
| 3 | Role prompting and instruction framing |
| 4 | Output format, structure, and control |
| 5 | Chaining, strategy, and advanced prompt thinking |

## How A Run Works

1. Enter your name and start the quest.
2. Play through 5 levels.
3. Complete 4 exercises in each level.
4. Read the explanation after every answer, especially when you miss one.
5. Finish all levels to see your final score, time, and rank.

## What You Practice

- Multiple choice: spot the better prompt or the missing detail.
- Free text: write a stronger prompt in your own words.
- Ordering: arrange prompt parts into the best sequence.
- Matching: connect prompt ingredients to the job each one does.

## How To Get Better Faster

- Add more direction when a prompt is vague.
- Name the audience when tone or difficulty matters.
- Ask for a format when you want structure.
- Add boundaries such as length, style, exclusions, or required elements.
- When the result is weak, refine the prompt instead of repeating it.

## Best Way To Use This Guide

Treat every question as a small lesson in prompt design. The most useful habit is not just getting the answer right, but noticing why one prompt works better than another. Better prompts usually become clearer, more specific, better structured, and easier for the AI to follow.
